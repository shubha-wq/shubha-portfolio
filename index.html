Marketing Executive · Fitness & Wellness

Sisters in Sweat

Brand strategy, visual identity and marketing systems built in-house for India's largest women-first movement community.

Brand Strategy Visual Identity Design Marketing Systems
Read the case study +
01, Context

Sisters in Sweat is India's largest women-first community for everything movement, sports, fitness, wellness, social and recreational. Started in 2017, SIS went on to receive funding from Rainmatter and get Nike onboard as a multi-year partner.

I joined as a Marketing Executive. Soon after starting, it was clear there was a lot more ground to cover than the title suggested, the marketing funnel, team co-ordination, visual identity and marketing narrative. So I put on multiple hats.

02, Problem
01
No brand strategy

With no formal brand strategy in place, the visual language across social media and other touchpoints had developed inconsistently. There was also no documented visual identity or brand guideline deck for the team to work from.

02
Partner brand attrition

Inconsistent visual language and content standards made it harder to meet partner expectations reliably, and one-off activations rarely converted into long-term partnerships.

03
Broken marketing funnel

The website predated the community's growth and was not built for conversion, so traffic rarely translated into members or event registrations. That left Instagram carrying most of the acquisition load.

04
Communication gaps

Without documented SOPs or a formalised workflow, deadlines were difficult to hold and a significant share of the team's time went into alignment meetings and rework.

03, Process

I introduced trackers, content calendars and reusable templates that improved day-to-day efficiency and reduced time spent in alignment meetings. After raising the gap in brand strategy and visual identity, we chose to build it in-house rather than engage an agency, I owned and executed that project end-to-end, delivering a full brand guideline deck. I also led the brand marketing charter, running collaborative campaigns with partners including Cava, Nike, Mille, Nykaa Wellness and In Her Interest by Zerodha.

04, Outcome

Sisters in Sweat now operates on a brand system built entirely in-house: a consistent visual identity across every partner collaboration, and workflows that keep the team focused on execution rather than admin. The content strategy work also helped secure the Nykaa Wellness partnership and bring an additional protein brand on board.

Creative Strategist · Jewellery & Lifestyle

Mohmani

Creative direction and content strategy for a homegrown jewellery label, holding onto whimsy while the numbers had to grow.

A niche following of ~3.3k, built on content, pop-ups and word of mouth.
Creative Direction Content Strategy Brand Storytelling
Read the case study +
01, Context

Mohmani is a small, homegrown jewellery label specialising in one-of-one pieces and custom orders. Over time it has grown into a content and culture-heavy brand, shaped as much by the founder's personal voice as by the jewellery itself. Through viral content, events and word-of-mouth discovery, it has built a niche following of around 3.3k people who connect with its whimsical world.

02, Problem

As Mohmani became more visible, the challenge was coherence rather than reach. With the brand developing across content, pop-ups and the founder's personal presence simultaneously, it needed creative direction clear enough to hold all three together, without forcing it into a conventional lifestyle or commercial mould.

03, Process

I put structure in place so creative decisions didn't feel random. Early on, the brand's core cues, whimsy, quirk and subcultural reference, were defined and used to guide everything. I set direction for visuals and tone, and built a content calendar so posting stayed consistent and intentional. Each collection started from a clear inspiration, shared with the audience through creative concepts rather than long explanations. Content pillars were built around the jewellery, the making process, everyday moments and the founder's voice, so the brand felt expressive rather than over-produced. Engagement, pop-ups and viral moments then told us what resonated, and the direction was refined against that while staying true to the niche.

04, Outcome

Mohmani now feels settled in its identity, less a brand chasing growth, more one that has found its place. The creative strategy brought clarity and continuity across content, community moments and product storytelling while keeping the brand's quirks intact. It also gave the founder a flexible framework to keep experimenting and showing up authentically without losing what makes the brand resonate.
