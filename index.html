My Journey

Learning is my default. I don't stay anywhere I'm not evolving.

I've always been drawn to extraordinary stories, unconventional approaches and tasteful visuals. My education is rooted in media & communications and my early career in PR — but somewhere along the way I got curious about the core of a business: the product.

Figuring out how product decisions tie to business outcomes gave my creativity a structure. Now I catch myself applying that systems thinking and user empathy every time I build a marketing narrative — which is, I think, the useful part.
